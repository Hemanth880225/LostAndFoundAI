🏛️ Police Stations & Custody

Police stations maintain official property custody books and secure storage for high-value items, passports, identity cards, and mobile devices. They provide official documentation for insurers and claimants.

🏫 Campus Security Desks

If you are a student or visitor on college property, the campus security command desk is the primary official repository for student items, backpacks, notebooks, and student ID cards.

📮 Post Office Caveat

Ordinary post offices do not automatically accept found items unless mailed with postage or officially coordinated. Always inquire directly with counter personnel before handing over any item.
