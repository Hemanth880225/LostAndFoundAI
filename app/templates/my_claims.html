How Claim Verification Works

1. When you identify your lost belonging in a found-item report, submit a claim with non-public details (serial numbers, private marks, or receipts).
2. Designated campus reviewers verify the details.
3. If more information is needed, an inquiry is posted to your claim dossier.
4. Once approved, you can complete physical handover at a designated campus desk.
