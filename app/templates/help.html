A LITTLE CLARITY

Good to know
before you reconnect.

Foundly helps people find possible connections. You stay in control of what happens next.

⌕

Matches are suggestions

AI compares report text, attributes, dates, locations, and images when available. A high score only means details are similar; it does not verify who owns an item.

♡

Keep private details private

Don’t post passwords, device codes, full identity numbers, or sensitive documents. Save one distinctive detail to confirm privately during a claim.

⌂

Arrange returns safely

Use a public, staffed location and bring someone you trust. Don’t share your home address or send money to recover an item.

✉

Claims are reviewed by people

Claim submissions are recorded as pending. This project does not yet include reviewer tools, claimant sign-in, or a private claims inbox.
