COMMUNITY & RECOVERY GUIDE

Good to know
before you reconnect.

Foundly connects honest finders, owners, and campus desks. Understand how our multimodal AI matching, safe handovers, and claims verification work.

✨

1. Multimodal AI Similarity

Our matching engine combines OpenAI CLIP visual embeddings with attribute analysis (category, color, shape, size, material, and distinctive marks like keychains or stickers). Match scores represent heuristic similarity, not definitive proof of ownership.

🔒

2. Keep Identifying Secrets Private

Never post passwords, device unlock patterns, full identity numbers, or sensitive financial documents in public listings. Keep one or two distinctive details private so you can verify ownership during a claim.

📍

3. Safe Handover Points

Never arrange meetings in private or secluded areas. Use our Safe Handover Directory to find verified campus security desks, official lost-property offices, and local police stations.

🛡️

4. Verified Claims & Review

Found items are reclaimed through verified claims. Reviewers inspect ownership details and supplemental evidence (invoices, receipts, serial numbers). All private documents are stored in secure directories outside public web roots.

🛺

5. Guest & Transit Recovery

Auto-rickshaw, taxi, and bus finders do not need an account or government ID to report a forgotten item. Finders receive a secure Case Reference and private secret key to coordinate with owners.

🏆

6. Honest Finder Rewards

To incentivize honest behavior, finders are eligible for value-based reward points (5 points = ₹1) based on verified item value. Points are credited to an immutable ledger only after confirmed physical return.
